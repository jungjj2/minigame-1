# Jason Jung - Minigame 1
## Devlog
Components, GameObjects, and Scenes are like parts of a movie. The components are the different things an actor has to do such as speaking, listening, moving, and doing actions like picking something up. The GameObjects are the actors themselves who are performing and doing all the components assigned to them. The scenes are the movie sets with the actors, background, and various objects that are on the set.
